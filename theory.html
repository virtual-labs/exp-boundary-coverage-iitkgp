Calculation of Boundary Coverage Probability            

Theory

Introduction:

The circular coverage region of a Base Station is the area defined by a certain radius R with the Base Station at the center where the mean signal level received at the mobile unit remains above a specified threshold with certain probability. The probability mentioned above is decided based on quality of service requirement. If there is no shadow fading then the radius R can be calculated for a region where the signal level crosses the threshold with certainty.

However in real conditions, shadow fading plays an important role in cellular network design. Due to the random variation of the signal strength received owing to shadow fade, one needs to find the probability with which received signal strength crosses the predicted threshold. The detail derivation in the context of % boundary coverage and% area coverage are given below:

1.1 % Boundary Coverage:-

The received signal power in log domain at a distance d from the Base Station is given by:

Pr(d)=Prˉ(d0)+10nplog⁡10(d0d)+xdBP_r(d) = \bar{P_r}(d_0) + 10 n_p \log_{10}\left(\frac{d_0}{d}\right) + x_{dB}Pr​(d)=Pr​ˉ​(d0​)+10np​log10​(dd0​​)+xdB​

Where,

  • xdBx_{dB}xdB​ represents shadow fading

  • xdBx_{dB}xdB​ is a random variable with Gaussian probability density function with mean Prˉ(d)\bar{P_r}(d)Pr​ˉ​(d) and standard deviation σxdB\sigma_{x_{dB}}σxdB​​.

The probability that the signal level crosses the certain sensitivity level γ\gammaγ is given by:

Prob[Pr(d)>γ]=∫γ∞p(x)dx\text{Prob}[P_r(d) > \gamma] = \int_{\gamma}^{\infty} p(x)dxProb[Pr​(d)>γ]=∫γ∞​p(x)dx

=1−∫−∞γp(x)dx= 1 - \int_{-\infty}^{\gamma} p(x)dx=1−∫−∞γ​p(x)dx

=1−Prob[Pr(d)<γ]= 1 - \text{Prob}[P_r(d) < \gamma]=1−Prob[Pr​(d)<γ]

=1−FPr(γ)= 1 - F_{P_r}(\gamma)=1−FPr​​(γ)

=1−[12+12erf(γ−Prˉ(d)2σxdB)]=12erfc(γ−Prˉ(d)2σxdB)= 1 - \left[\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\text{erf}\left(\frac{\gamma - \bar{P_r}(d)}{\sqrt{2}\sigma_{x_{dB}}}\right)\right] = \frac{1}{2}\text{erfc}\left(\frac{\gamma - \bar{P_r}(d)}{\sqrt{2}\sigma_{x_{dB}}}\right)=1−[21​+21​erf(2​σxdB​​γ−Pr​ˉ​(d)​)]=21​erfc(2​σxdB​​γ−Pr​ˉ​(d)​)

=Q(γ−Prˉ(d)σxdB)= Q\left(\frac{\gamma - \bar{P_r}(d)}{\sigma_{x_{dB}}}\right)=Q(σxdB​​γ−Pr​ˉ​(d)​)

The % area coverage is determined by the radius RγR_{\gamma}Rγ​ at which the signal level γ\gammaγ exceeds the sensitivity level with probability ProbRγ\text{Prob}_{R_{\gamma}}ProbRγ​​ which is the likelihood of coverage at the cell boundary with d=Rγd=R_{\gamma}d=Rγ​.

Fuγ=1πRγ2∫Prob[Pr(d)>γ]dAF_u^{\gamma} = \frac{1}{\pi R_{\gamma}^2} \int \text{Prob}[P_r(d) > \gamma] dAFuγ​=πRγ2​1​∫Prob[Pr​(d)>γ]dA
